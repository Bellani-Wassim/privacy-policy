Implace - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Implace ("the app") replaces the sky, or the background behind a person, in a photo with art from Wplace (wplace.live). Implace is an unofficial fan app. It is not affiliated with Wplace. It is free, has no ads, and has no accounts.

Short version

The app collects no personal data, has no account, no analytics and no ads. Your photos never leave your phone.

Photos and camera

The app uses the camera, or a photo you choose from your gallery, only to make the picture on your phone. The photo is processed on the device. It is not uploaded, sent to any server, or shared. Nothing is saved to your gallery unless you press the Download button.

Location

If you choose "Your location", the app asks for permission to read your location. It is used only on the phone to find which Wplace map tile covers your area. The app does not store your location and does not send your coordinates anywhere. You can instead paste a Wplace link or pick a city, and then no location permission is needed.

Information sent over the internet

To show the map, the app downloads a Wplace tile image from https://backend.wplace.live. The request contains the number of the tile (an area of tens of kilometres), so it reveals an approximate area, and, as with any internet request, your IP address is visible to that server. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). This is used only to load the map and is not used for analytics, advertising or personalisation. The tiles are saved temporarily on your phone and are deleted when you leave the app or return to the home screen. The Wplace service has its own privacy policy.

Links

The Paint button and the link in the About dialog open wplace.live in your browser. What happens there is governed by that website's own policies.

Data we collect, sell or share

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. There is no server operated by the developer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to delete. You can remove everything the app keeps on your phone by uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published on this page.
